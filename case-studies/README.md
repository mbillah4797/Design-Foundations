# Case studies

Four anonymised stories from our own work. Each shows several rules acting together, and each ends with what a stranger can take from it. Names, products, identifiers and numbers that would identify anyone have been removed; the method is intact.

| Story | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [1. Proving that nothing visible changed](01-proving-nothing-visible-changed.md) | Two independent comparison tools that must agree, repeated refute passes, counterexamples kept as permanent tests, and an owner's decision built into the release machinery. |
| [2. Three stops that were the system working](02-three-stops-that-were-the-system-working.md) | A build that aborted safely on known noise, an automatic merge that collided with a release, and a correct release rolled back on purpose. |
| [3. A design call made by blind panels, a census and looking](03-a-design-call-made-by-looking.md) | Getting a design judgement that is not your own: other model families, blind ratings with the incumbent hidden, floors versus taste rules, and a human decision made by looking. |
| [4. The caveat at the bottom of a green report](04-the-caveat-at-the-bottom-of-a-green-report.md) | A correction volunteered to the decider, and why the fix was a change to the machinery rather than a stronger sentence in a brief. |
